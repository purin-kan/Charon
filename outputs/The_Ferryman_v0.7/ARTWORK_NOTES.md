# Artwork and font provenance

Existing project images are reused from `outputs/The_Ferryman_v0.6/assets/art/`. Existing Farmer v7, Blacksmith v4, Priest normal v2 and exhausted v3 proposals were copied from the October 30 catalog's original local images. Their identities and hashes are recorded in `verification/artwork.json`. No new artwork or personal reference photo was generated, uploaded or embedded in this trial.

The repository's historical rights records remain in `outputs/The_Ferryman_v0.6/art/` and `LICENSES.md`. Copying these project assets does not assert a new third-party license, exclusive ownership or final team art approval. Characters and locations without selected images use initial/text placeholders.

Bitstream Vera fonts retain their supplied license at `assets/fonts/bitstream-vera-license.txt`. The trial makes no remote font request. No new open-source license or external-publication permission is granted by this local package.
