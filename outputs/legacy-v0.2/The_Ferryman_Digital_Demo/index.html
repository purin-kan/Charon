Setup and testing notes

A SHORT ORIENTATION

Keep the ferry running.

Complete six crossings and pay 3 obols after crossings 3 and 6. Start with 2 obols and 3 Standing.

  1. Choose passengers. Their seat costs must total 4 or less. An empty boat is legal.
  2. Choose a memory, optionally. Recall up to two, play at most one for 1 Standing. Some protect a waiting soul from its normal refusal.
  3. Choose a route. Elysium costs 1 obol with 0 base pressure. Asphodel is free with 1 pressure. Tartarus is free with 2 pressure and pays 1 service obol on a nonempty arrival.
  4. Read the preview and depart. Pay costs, absorb pressure, collect arrival rewards, pay any quota, then advance refusals.
Standing must cover the river's pressure. If it cannot, the run ends before rewards. Exactly enough Standing is sufficient. Fares earned later cannot fund departure costs.

Souls left ashore gain 1 refusal. If both partners were available and only one boards, the waiting partner gains 1 extra refusal. At 3 refusals, a soul becomes a persistent wraith: +1 pressure starting next crossing.

New memories retain their source soul. The queue and discard are never shuffled. Acquired memories enter the queue by soul ID; cleanup discards the played card first, then the rest of the hand left-to-right.

This follows the base paper trial in workbook v0.2. Optional events, upgrades and destination wishes are off. The journal is a record of this run, not evidence of balance.
