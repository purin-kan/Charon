## Printing and assembly

1. Print the play kit at **Actual size / 100%**, A4 landscape, single-sided. Measure the 50 mm check on page 1 before printing the whole set.
2. Cut the 12 soul fronts on pages 2-3 and their 12 matching wraith backs on pages 4-5. Match by source ID and mount together or place back-to-back in sleeves. Automatic duplex alignment is not required.
3. Cut the 12 memory instances on pages 6-7. Each keeps its source soul ID. For S01, S02, S11 and S12, mark one acquired variant in pencil: R05 joint or R06 solo. Never put both variants into the deck for a single delivery.
4. Cut out the three route panels on page 8, or display the entire page. Keep the boat, shore and memory mats on pages 9-11 whole.
5. Cut the resource squares, small seat/general markers, and six-millimetre refusal dots on page 12. Paper clips or your own small counters can replace refusal dots. The token quantities are physical supplies, not rule limits. Keep the six-space crossing strip together.
6. Keep page 13 with the facilitator. Use the workbook's section 17A and C01 checklist for rehearsal and showcase readiness. This pack does not certify that rehearsal is complete.

Soul and memory cards measure 63 x 88 mm. Route panels measure 85 x 165 mm. Blank vector templates retain these sizes. Card position has no gameplay effect; sum the printed seat costs and use the separate capacity markers.

The artwork boards use full-color dark backgrounds. The play kit uses mostly white card faces. If printing economically, project the art boards and print the play kit. Readability does not depend on color alone.

## Setup and reset

Crossing 1. Four seats. Two obols. Three Standing. Supply ordered S01 to S12, with S01 to S05 on the shore and all refusals at zero. Empty wraith area, delivery history, memory queue, hand and discard. All three routes open. Optional modules and events off. Clear old pencil variant marks and temporary effects when resetting. Keep observations separate from the game state.

## Visual proposal

The direction is **ink and lantern light**: charcoal river tones, parchment, warm gold, quiet olive for Elysium and ember clay for Tartarus. The concept illustrations were created using the built-in image-generation tool. The card layouts, typography and vector templates are separately authored. No historical authenticity or final design approval is claimed. Mythological places are creative game interpretations; route prices and rewards do not assign moral worth to souls.

The images remain at their original generated resolution, 1536 x 1024 pixels. They are suitable for screen presentation and prototype-scale print placement; a large production poster may need new higher-resolution artwork. Rules are typeset separately from the images.
