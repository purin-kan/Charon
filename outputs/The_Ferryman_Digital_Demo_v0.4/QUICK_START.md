# The Ferryman v0.4 quick start

Open `index.html` and choose **Begin the journey**, or **Resume** for your saved run. Keep the folder and its assets together. No install or account is needed.

You are an apprentice ferryman. Carry souls, collect their memories and keep the lantern alive through an endless series of trips. Start with **2 light**, a maximum of **6**, and **4 seats**.

1. **Choose passengers.** Check seats, wishes, abilities and the waiting-shore anger forecast. You may undo boarding before departure.
2. **Choose a route.** Fog costs light. Route cards show current fog before memory protection. You can visit several destinations per trip, but cannot revisit a stop.
3. **Choose a memory, or none.** A memory costs no light. Some also protect a waiting soul from normal anger. The review shows its effect before you spend it.
4. **Confirm the crossing.** Back changes your plan freely. Exactly 0 light survives. Fog greater than your light ends the run before arrival and rewards.
5. **Choose who disembarks.** Select everyone before confirming. Every delivered soul gives a memory; a matched wish restores 1 light. A matching linked pair delivered together gives Joined Memories. Passengers you keep aboard continue to another destination.
6. **Return with an empty boat.** You must deliver everyone first. At the final unvisited destination, all remaining passengers must disembark. Returning raises waiting anger, restores 1 light and refills the shore.

Waiting souls normally gain 1 anger per return. Leaving one linked partner waiting adds another 1. Memory protection cancels normal anger only. At anger 3, a waiting soul becomes a wraith, adding 1 fog to future crossings. At the route step, you may release a wraith for 2 light.

Your hand draws to three after delivery, Haven arrival or return. Play at most one card per crossing. Discards recycle. Haven restores 1 light after arrival, with no boarding or delivery there.

For a first trip, Mother, Child and Merchant fit in four seats. Take the linked pair to Elysium, then use the memories they leave to plan the Merchant's next crossing to Tartarus. Watch the preview and choose a waiting target if you want protection on return. This is an introduction, not a claimed best strategy.

Use **Guide & save** for help, JSON export/import and New Run. Progress saves locally after each committed step. Export before moving computers or clearing browser data. v0.3 saves cannot be imported. New Run asks before replacing current progress.

Use Tab and Enter/Space for buttons, arrow keys for a memory target, and Escape to close the guide. The light/boat summary stays visible while scrolling. “Trip information” keeps the boat, wishes, waiting anger, wraiths and visited stops available.

Read [full rules](RULES.md) for all abilities, cycle escalation and event spoilers. This version has no hull, obols, quotas, reprimands, repairs or paid calming. The v0.3 paper kit uses different rules; a v0.4 kit is deferred until this flow is tried.
