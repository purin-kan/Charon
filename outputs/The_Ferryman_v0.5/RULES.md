# The Ferryman v0.5 rules

Provisional single-player workshop rules. See DESIGN_DECISIONS.md for feedback sources and adaptations.

## Objective and setup

Strictly one player. Finish four trips with at least eight fulfilled wishes and survive the final return. If no souls remain at the shore or in the arrival supply after a survived return, the night ends early with the same eight-wish requirement. Target duration is 20-30 minutes; it has not been measured with humans. Start light at 5 (maximum 6), trip at 1, anger at 0, wishes at 0 and boat moves at 0. No memories, Wraiths or Passage. Shuffle the 12 Arrival tickets, 10 Routes and 4 Events separately, face down. Keep the 14 Soul cards and 13 Memory cards in face-up reserves. Draw Arrival tickets until at least five souls wait or the supply is empty. A pair can bring the shore to six. Move the named Soul cards to the shore and write a waiting deadline on each: current anger +5, or +3 for Fool. Used Arrival tickets never return. Reveal two Route cards before boarding.

## Boarding and route offers

Choose at least one waiting soul within four seats. Achilles takes two seats; all others take one. Narrow Channel reduces the boat to three seats for that trip. Boarding and unboarding are free and reversible before the first move; retain each soul's original waiting deadline. No boarding happens away from shore. Soul cards remain visible in both media. Choose one of the two Routes. Both offered Routes are discarded after the crossing, except a chosen Sanctuary is retired permanently. After a stop, reveal two new Routes only if passengers remain. Whenever the Route pile runs out while dealing, shuffle its discard pile and finish the two-card offer. Unchosen Sanctuary stays in circulation. Revisiting a named destination is allowed if its Route appears again. With an empty boat, return is mandatory as the next move; no more outward detours.

## Memories and crossing cost

Before any move, including return, optionally play one earned Memory. It has only its printed effect and is removed from play after use. Light restoration happens before costs and never exceeds six. Calm adds two to one waiting soul's deadline; it does not prevent fog or change a boat timer. A fog memory reduces only fog, to a minimum of zero. Fog equals Route base + night pressure + number of existing Wraiths + one if both soldiers are aboard + event fog - memory prevention. Night pressure is zero on trips 1-2 and one on trips 3-4, including returns, Haven and Sanctuary. Killer drains one additional light before fog on every move aboard; a shield cannot block this drain. If available light is less than drain plus fog, lose immediately before advancing clocks or arriving. Exactly zero light after payment survives. Committed memories are spent even on a losing move.

## Shared anger and the last delivery chance

After a surviving move, advance the shared anger by one. A waiting soul whose written deadline is now reached becomes a Wraith. This is the paper form of +1 anger per move: only the shared anger marker moves, not a marker on every soul. A boarded soul ignores its waiting deadline. Advance boat moves by one on outward moves only. Then resolve delivery or Haven. After delivery, every tainted passenger still aboard on boat move two becomes a Ship Wraith. On boat move three, all remaining passengers become Ship Wraiths. The arrival is their last chance to leave. There is no fourth outward move. Normal and Ship Wraiths have the same ongoing effect: each adds one fog to every later move. Keep their Soul cards in the Wraith area; no removal action exists. Newly formed Wraiths do not increase the cost of the move just survived.

## Deliveries, wishes and the hand limit

At Elysium, Asphodel, Tartarus or Styx, choose any subset of passengers, including none, to deliver in one action. All these destinations accept all souls. A wish matches only the destination printed on the Soul card. Each matched soul scores one wish and earns its corresponding Memory from the reserve, except Po Din earns no Memory. A landing with at least one matched delivery restores one light total, regardless of how many wishes matched. An unmatched delivery grants no score, light or Memory, but safely removes that passenger from the boat. Delivered souls never return. After the expiry check, if more than three memories are held, choose and permanently discard excess cards before making another move. There is no Memory draw pile or reshuffle. Unused memories persist between trips.

## Haven, Sanctuary and the family quest

Haven has base fog zero. Pay all added fog and Killer drain before gaining one light, capped at six. Deliver nobody there. Anger, boat moves and passenger expiry all apply. Sanctuary has base fog zero and treats every delivered soul as matching its wish, including Achilles. It grants no light or Memories and is retired after use. Ordinary pressure and both clocks still apply. Delivering Mother and Child together in one action with both wishes matched, at Elysium or Sanctuary, unlocks Passage once. Before a later move, while boarding or choosing a route, spend Passage to send one waiting soul directly to its printed wish. Gain one wish only, with no light or Memory; no clock advances. Passage cannot target a passenger aboard, a Wraith, a delivered soul or someone who has not arrived.

## Return and events

The boat must be empty to return, and at least one outward move must have happened. Return is a move with base fog zero. Play at most one Memory, pay night pressure and existing Wraith fog, advance anger and resolve waiting deadlines. Do not advance boat moves or recover light. After a survived return, record a completed trip. After trip four, end and check the eight-wish target. Otherwise advance the trip and reset boat moves to zero. Clear the prior Event. On trips two and four only, reveal one Event before refilling. Its face-up text remains available. Restless Shore subtracts one from every existing waiting deadline, resolves any now-due Wraiths immediately, and does not affect new arrivals. Then refill to at least five waiting souls, allowing six for a pair, and reveal two Routes. Other Events apply only to the new trip. No game master, timer, die, unlock ledger or hidden passenger information is required.

## Paper example and reset

Example: anger 2, light 4, trip 2, boat move 0. Killer and Merchant are aboard; Po Din waits with deadline 3. Choose Ashen Gate (base 1) with no Memory, no active Event and no Wraiths. Pay 1 Killer drain +1 fog, leaving light 2. Advance anger to 3 and boat moves to 1. Po Din now becomes a Wraith; it did not tax this move. Deliver Killer and Merchant to Tartarus: gain 2 wishes, their 2 Memories, and only 1 light, ending at light 3. The empty return now costs 1 Wraith fog. Reset: return all Soul and Memory cards to their reserves, restore the retired Sanctuary to Routes, collect all Arrival and Event tickets, erase deadlines, reshuffle the three random piles and reset trackers. No consumable component is required beyond erasable pencil marks.
