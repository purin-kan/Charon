## **Combined Oct 1 feedback**

1. **The game is too easy.** All four people said this.  
   * Light reaches 6/6 early and stays there, and extra Light is wasted (Goya, Boss).  
   * Runs last 20+ cycles and it's hard to lose (Goya).  
   * Decisions aren't hard to make (Tinpat).  
   * Too many resources are handed out (Tinpat).  
   * **Suggested fixes:**  
     * Increase fog each cycle or in later cycles (Goya, Boss).  
     * Reduce Light recovery (Goya).  
     * Give extra Light another use (Boss).  
     * Hand out fewer resources (Tinpat).  
2. **Haven is free.** It gives \+1 Light at no cost, which makes it an easy way out (Title, Boss). Boss suggests costing it more fog or more Anger.  
3. **Memories do too much.**  
   * Each memory should have one effect (Title).  
   * Joined Memory and Recollection both prevent fog *and* protect a waiting soul, so Anger almost never builds. In two cycles only one soul reached Anger 1, so make players choose one effect per play (Boss).  
   * Memories should protect less (Goya).  
4. **The game is too long.**  
   * It takes too long before you can lose (Goya).  
   * Each round takes a lot of time (Tinpat).  
   * Goya floats a clearer win condition or ending point, marked "(?)".  
5. **Some things are confusing.**  
   * You can't see passenger info once you're en route (Title). He also notes that remembering it could count as a skill.  
   * Effects mention souls that haven't arrived yet, and the soldier conflict never triggered because they arrived in different cycles. Boss suggests labelling them "inactive" or "not yet arrived."  
6. **There's no hook.** Once you understand the mechanics, there's nothing left to discover (Title).

**How Guy's new game idea lines up with this feedback:**

| Problem | Guy's answer |
| ----- | ----- |
| Too easy / Light always full | Random routes plus \+1 Anger to waiting souls on every move (he says this fixes full Light), Killer, Ship Wraith |
| Anger does nothing | Same Anger rule, plus the Fool |
| No hook / repetitive | Random routes, events every 5–10 cycles, quests, tainted souls, special destinations (Achilles → Styx) |
| Memories too complex | Simpler, character-themed memories, matching Title's one-effect idea |
| **Not addressed** | Game length, Haven's cost, passenger info, "not yet arrived" labels |

## **Conflicts**

1. **An endless run, or an ending?**

   * **Endless:** Title's argument assumes "we are aiming for infinite run." Guy's idea is built on it: once everyone is delivered you go back for more souls, and new content unlocks at cycles 5 and 10\.  
   * **Shorter, or a clear ending:** Goya (20+ cycles is too long; add an ending point) and Tinpat (cut rounds).  
   * **Middle ground:** stay endless but make failure come sooner, which is Goya's main point. The catch is that if runs end sooner, few players will reach Guy's cycle-10 content (10 routes, 14 souls).  
2. **A free, safe stop.**

   * **Against:** Title says a stop that costs no Light breaks an infinite run. Boss says Haven needs a cost.  
   * **For:** Guy proposes a free stage where everyone gets their wish at no cost.  
3. **More Light.**

   * Goya and Boss say Light is already stuck at full and the extra is wasted.  
   * Guy's example memory is "+1 Light," which makes that worse. Boss's idea of giving extra Light another use is the alternative.  
4. **Round length vs. per-move tracking.**

   * Tinpat says rounds already take too long.  
   * Guy adds several things to track every move:  
     * \+1 Anger to every waiting soul on every move.  
     * A turn timer on each passenger for Ship Wraith.  
     * The Killer draining Light on every move.  
   * Guy also says the design must work on physical paper, where each of these adds steps to every round.  
5. **Harder decisions vs. fewer choices.**

   * Tinpat wants harder decisions, and Goya wants players to lose from bad decisions.  
   * Guy replaces route choice with random routes. Every 5–10 cycles the master also picks your souls, so there's no choice at all.  
   * His wish-fork idea (choosing who gets their wish) does add a hard decision, so that part fits the feedback.  
6. **Random arrivals vs. pairs.**

   * Boss found the soldier conflict never happened because the pair arrived apart.  
   * Guy's random souls and his mother–child quest (with both marked rare) make pairs meet even less often.  
   * Unless pairs are drawn together, both the soldier conflict and the mother–child quest will rarely trigger, and more effects will point at souls that aren't there.  
7. **Resources.**

   * Tinpat says too many are handed out.  
   * Guy adds more memory types and more souls over time, plus a quest reward that sends a soul to its destination instantly.  
   * His Po Din, a soul who leaves no memory, goes the other way and fits Tinpat's point.

**Smaller differences:**

* **Memory fix:** Title wants one fixed effect per card, while Boss keeps both effects but lets you use only one per play.  
* **Anger fix:** Boss weakens memory protection, while Guy adds the Anger increase on every move. Doing both at once could overshoot.  
* **Passenger info:** Title argues both sides himself (show it anytime vs. remembering it is a skill), and it isn't settled.
