P001 Game Guide Demo
P002 Premise
P003 You play Charon's apprentice. Your job is to ferry souls across the river and drop each one off where it wants to go.
P004 Key terms
P005 Round: one stop on the river. The river splits into 2 destinations, and you travel to one of them.
P006 Cycle: one full trip. It starts when you pick up souls at the shore and ends when your boat is empty. Then you return to the shore for new arrivals.
P007 Light: your health. If light reaches 0, you lose. Start at 6.
P008 Fog: each destination has a fog value. When you arrive there, you lose that much Light.
P009 Seats: your boat has 4 seats. Most souls take 1 seat, and some take 2.
P010 Anger: how long a soul has been kept waiting. Too much Anger turns a soul into a wraith (see Anger below).
P011 Memory: a card you earn by delivering a soul to its preferred destination. Each memory has a special effect.
P012 How a cycle plays
P013 New map. A river map with a set path is randomly generated for this cycle. 
P014 Choose passengers. At the shore, fill up to 4 seats with souls. Each soul has a preferred destination, and you need to deliver all of them before they turn into wraiths. Souls you don't pick stay on the shore.
P015 Pick a destination. At the start of each round, choose 1 of 2 destinations. When you arrive, you lose Light equal to that destination's fog.
P016 Drop off souls (optional). A soul can only be dropped off at its preferred destination. When you drop one off, you get its memory. You can also keep a soul on board and drop it off later, for example to complete a quest in the right order.
P017 End of round. Every soul still on your boat gains +1 Ship Anger.
P018 Repeat steps 3–5 until your boat is empty.
P019 Return to the shore. Every soul still waiting on the shore gains +1 Shore Anger. New souls arrive, and the next cycle starts from step 1.

P042 Quests
P043 Some souls have a quest. Completing it gives you a special memory, which is stronger than a normal one.
P044 Tainted souls
P045 Every 3 rounds, a tainted soul is placed on your boat, and you can't refuse it. It carries a debuff that stays active until it leaves your boat. Otherwise it works like any other soul: it has a preferred destination and gains Ship Anger.
