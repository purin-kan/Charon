# Licenses and provenance

`assets/fonts/bitstream-vera-license.txt` is the supplied license for bundled Vera fonts. Those local fonts remove any need for a remote font host.

`assets/REUSED_PROVENANCE.json` records exact hashes and earlier source records for unchanged project artwork. These are project-supplied assets; no new third-party license, exclusive ownership or final art approval is inferred by copying them. A's 23 delivered originals, provenance, generation prompts and rights records are in `art/ASSET_MANIFEST.json`, `art/GENERATION_RECORDS.json` and `art/LICENSES.md`. Print font licenses are also bundled under `tools/print/fonts/`.

The two supplied Word guides are unchanged provenance copies, not the reconciled player rules. Their source identities and conflict-resolution records are included under `source/`. The project/team retains its existing rights in code, text and assets; this local preparation does not add an open-source license or authorize external publication.
