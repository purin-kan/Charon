## How memories work

- **Earning:** delivering a soul adds its memory to the bottom of the draw pile.
- **Hand:** you draw back up to three cards after each delivery, Haven arrival or return to shore.
- **Using:** one card per crossing at most. A used card goes to the discard pile, which is shuffled back in when the draw pile runs out.
- **Protecting a soul:** Recollection and Joined Memory can also shield one waiting soul from normal anger on your return.
