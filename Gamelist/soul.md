## Linked partners

- **Mother and Child** (S01, S02)
- **Musician and Listener** (S11, S12)

Deliver both partners from the same group together to give each a Joined Memory. Delivered apart, each gives a Faint Memory, and a partner left waiting on the shore gains extra anger.

## Rivals

- **Red Soldier and Blue Soldier** (S04, S06): both aboard from the same group adds 1 fog. The Accord memory cancels it.
